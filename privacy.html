Promote Ready Now Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Instructions & Support Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Short version: Promote Ready Now is designed to work without creating an account, and your study activity is intended to remain on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Promote Ready Now does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, mailing address, or other personal profile information in order to use the app.

The app may store study-related information locally on your device, such as:

  • Your selected study rank, such as E5 or E6.
  • Quiz and test statistics.
  • Missed questions.
  • Flagged questions.
  • Study and testing preferences.
  • Appearance preferences, such as light or dark mode.

2. Local Data Storage

Study progress and preferences are intended to be stored locally on your device using Apple-provided storage mechanisms. Promote Ready Now does not require a cloud account for normal use.

If you delete the app, reset app data, or erase your device, locally stored information may be permanently lost.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Promote Ready Now is not designed to track you across apps or websites and does not sell personal information.

If Apple or the App Store provides standard crash, installation, or diagnostic information to the developer, that information is governed by Apple's own privacy practices and platform settings. Such information may be used only to improve app stability, compatibility, and performance.

4. Third-Party Services

Promote Ready Now may provide links to external websites, such as support, privacy, or informational resources. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of those external websites or services apply.

5. Children's Privacy

Promote Ready Now is intended for users preparing for professional military promotion testing and is not directed toward children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to design the app so that study information remains on the user's device. However, no software, device, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. U.S. Government and Military Disclaimer

Promote Ready Now is an independent educational study application. It is not an official product of, endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with the United States Air Force, Department of Defense, or any other U.S. Government agency.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or improvements. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
