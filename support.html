Promote Ready Now is a focused study and practice app for users preparing for E5 or E6 promotion testing. It includes study questions, custom tests, missed-question review, flagged-question review, and progress statistics.

Getting Started

  1. Open Promote Ready Now.
  2. Select the rank you are studying for: E5 or E6.
  3. Use the home screen to begin studying, take a custom test, review missed questions, or review flagged questions.
  4. Your study statistics are tracked automatically on your device.

Study Mode

Study Mode is designed for question-by-question review.

  1. Open Study Mode from the home screen.
  2. Read the full question and select one of the four answer choices.
  3. Review the explanation when shown.
  4. Use the flag option to mark any question you want to revisit later.

Custom Tests

Custom Test lets you build a practice test using the available test-size options.

  1. Open Custom Test.
  2. Select the number of questions you want to answer, such as 20, 35, 50, or 100.
  3. Enable Random if you want the app to randomize the question selection.
  4. If available in your version, choose whether correct answers are shown while testing.
  5. Complete the test and review your results.

Flagged Questions

You can flag questions during study or testing when you want to return to them later.

  • Flagged questions remain available in the Flagged Questions section until cleared.
  • If you attempt to finish a test while questions are still flagged, the app may prompt you to review them before ending the test.
  • You may choose to review the flagged questions or continue and finish the test.

Missed Questions

Questions answered incorrectly may be saved to the Missed Questions section so you can concentrate on material that needs more review.

Use this section regularly to reinforce weak areas before taking another custom test.

Study Rank

Promote Ready Now supports separate study content for E5 and E6.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Change Study Rank.
  3. Select E5 or E6.
  4. Tap Save to confirm, or Cancel to leave the current selection unchanged.

Settings

Settings may include options for appearance, testing preferences, statistics, and stored question history.

Changes to general settings can be saved using the Save button. Actions that permanently clear study data, such as clearing flagged questions, clearing missed questions, or resetting quiz statistics, require confirmation before the data is removed.

Light and Dark Appearance

If appearance controls are enabled in your version of the app, you can select the display style that is easiest for you to read. The app is designed to keep questions, answer choices, and explanations readable in both light and dark appearances.

Statistics

The home screen may display study statistics such as questions studied, questions answered, correct and incorrect answers, or other progress indicators. These statistics are intended for personal study tracking only.

Resetting Data

When you choose an action such as clearing flagged questions, clearing missed questions, or resetting E5/E6 quiz statistics, Promote Ready Now will ask you to confirm the action before removing the stored data.

Troubleshooting

My questions or statistics do not look right

Confirm that the correct study rank is selected. E5 and E6 use separate study content and may maintain separate statistics.

I cannot find a question I flagged

Open the Flagged Questions section and confirm you are viewing the same study rank that was active when the question was flagged.

I changed a setting but it did not apply

Return to Settings, make the desired change, and tap Save before leaving the screen.

I deleted or reinstalled the app

Study data stored only on the device may not be recoverable after deletion or reinstallation.
